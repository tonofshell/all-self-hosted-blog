# All Self Hosted

Hey there! This blog follows my ongoing attempt to reclaim control over the services I depend on, by moving them off corporate platforms and onto hardware I own and maintain.

This currently includes:

- AI/LLMs
- Photo backup and sharing
- Media streaming
- Shared file storage

...with ambitious plans for even more! (We'll see how long it takes to get there...) Soliloquies on Linux, FOSS, infrastructure-as-code, and other tangential topics are also fairly likely.

My goal is to post once a month as life allows. In the meantime, feel free to sound off in the [GitHub discussions section](https://github.com/tonofshell/all-self-hosted-blog/discussions) with your experiences. Happy hosting!
